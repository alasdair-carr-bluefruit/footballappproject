Getting started

From a new account to your first match in about ten minutes.

  1. Sign in. Level is invite-only. Open your invite link, enter your email, and we'll send you a sign-in link. Tap it and confirm. No password to remember.
  2. Set up your team. Give your team a name (and a badge if you like). Coach more than one side? You can add more teams later.
  3. Add your squad. For each player, tick the positions they're happy in and give a rough skill rating from 1 to 5. Only you see the ratings. A shirt number is optional. Only switch on Keeper only? for a child who never plays outfield.
  4. Create a match. Pick your format (5v5, 7v7 or 9v9) and formation. Leave the playing-time slider on Equal for most weeks. Level remembers your settings for next time.
  5. Pick who's coming. Untick anyone who isn't playing and say why. A rested player is owed time in later matches. An ill or away player isn't.
  6. Check the plan. Level shows who plays where in every part of the match, and how much time each child gets. Want a change? Tap Tinker, swap players, and Level re-balances the rest.
  7. Run the match. Tap Start Match on the day. The clock runs, the plan moves on as you go, and you can record goals. Tap a player for an injury and pick who comes on. Nobody else moves.
  8. Full time. You get a match report to share with parents, and the minutes count towards your season stats.

Questions

Getting going

How do I put Level on my phone's home screen?

On iPhone, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen. It then opens like any other app.

My sign-in email hasn't arrived.

Give it a minute and check your spam or junk folder. Each link works once, so if you've already tapped it, ask for a new one.

Your plan

Can I change the plan Level makes?

Yes, any time. Tap Tinker to swap players around. Level keeps your changes and re-balances what's left so playing time stays fair.

What's the difference between rested and unavailable?

Rested is your choice, so Level makes sure that child catches up on time in later matches. Unavailable (ill, injured or away) doesn't count against anyone.

Who can see the skill ratings?

Only the head coach. Assistant coaches and parents never see them. They're only used to keep each part of the match evenly matched.

On matchday

A child gets hurt during the match. What do I do?

Tap the player and choose injury. You pick who comes on, and the rest of the plan stays the same so nobody else gets moved.

Two of us have the app open on matchday. Who's in charge?

One phone runs the match at a time, so there's one score and one clock. Anyone else watches live, and can tap Take control if they're taking over.

Coaches & teams

Can another coach help me?

Yes. Go to Settings → Assistant coaches and create an invite link for them. Assistants can see your plans, match reports and stats, and plan and run matches of their own. You can also hand one of your matches to them.

How do I hand the team over to someone else?

Invite them as an assistant first. Then go to Settings → Assistant coaches and tap Make head coach next to their name. A team has one head coach, so you'll stay on as their assistant. You can leave the team after that if you want to.

I coach more than one team.

Tap your team name at the top of the screen and choose Add a team. Each team's players and matches stay separate, and you switch between them from the same place.

Can I run a tournament?

Yes. Create a tournament instead of a match and add each game to it. Level keeps playing time fair across the whole day, not just each game.

The app & your data

The app looks out of date.

Go to Settings → App → Check for updates. This clears the old version and reloads the latest one.

I've found a bug.

Tap Report a bug in the app and tell us what happened, or email hello@keepthingslevel.com.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Clear squad & data. It permanently deletes your players, matches and tournaments. For anything else, email us.

About Level

How come Level is free?

I built Level because I wanted it myself, as a coach and as a club welfare officer, where I saw how hard it is to get fair playing time right every week. Once it worked for my team, I wanted any grassroots coach to be able to use it. There are no adverts and your data is never sold. If you'd like to help, a tip or a sponsorship covers the running costs (hosting, the database and sign-in emails) and keeps it free for everyone.
