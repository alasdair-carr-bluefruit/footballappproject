FA FutureFit 2026/27: what changes for grassroots youth coaches

Published 23 November 2026 · Level

From the start of the 2026/27 season, youth football in England is played under The FA's FutureFit changes. Every format now covers two age groups, U7s play 3v3, full 11v11 doesn't start until U14, and the laws are tailored to each format. Here's what changes, straight from The FA's own guide, and what it means for match day.

The new formats by age group

The FA has lined the formats up with primary and secondary school, so each one now covers two age groups:

3v3 is the headline change. In The FA's words it is “the entry format for players at U7s so they get to be more involved in the play”, and 11v11 “is introduced at U14s so players get to play smaller formats for longer.”

What if my team is mid-way through a format?

Teams move into the two-year cycle as they age up. The FA's own example: a team that played 5v5 as U7s in 2025/26 carries on playing 5v5 in 2026/27, plays a final season of 5v5 in 2027/28, then moves up to 7v7 in 2028/29 and continues in two-year steps from there. Your league will confirm exactly how it's applying this to your age group.

Two of these change how a game looks more than any other:

  • Sideline restarts. In 3v3, 5v5 and 7v7, a pass-in or dribble-in replaces the throw-in. Opponents stand at least three metres back in 3v3 and five yards back in 5v5 and 7v7, and you can't score directly from a pass-in.
  • The retreat line. At a goal-line restart, the team without the ball drops back into its own half until the ball is in play. The FA says this “increases passing sequences and technical involvement” instead of a ‘kick and chase’ game.

What The FA says about playing time

This is the line every coach should know. Under the laws table, The FA's guide says:

“For u7, all children play (no subs). For u8 and above, equal playing time (at least 50%) is the best practice recommendation.”

England Football, FutureFit: Know Your Format — The How-to Guide (February 2026), p.7

So from U8 upwards the bar is clear. Equal time is best practice, and no child should get less than half the match. It's a recommendation rather than a law, but it's the standard your club, your league and your parents will measure you against.

What it means for your match-day rotation

FutureFit changes the numbers behind your team sheet. A few things worth planning for:

  • Matches are shorter than many coaches are used to. A 40-minute 5v5 game with 8 children gives each child an equal share of exactly 25 minutes. With 10, it's 20 minutes, which is exactly the 50% line. See the full game-time table by age group.
  • Rolling subs make it easy to drift. You can change players whenever you like, which is great. But without a plan, the same few children end up on the bench every time the game gets tight. Deciding your changes before kick-off is the simplest fix.
  • Squad size matters more. If your match-day squad is more than double the number on the pitch, the 50% recommendation can't be met for everyone. That's worth knowing before you pick a squad of eleven for a 5v5 game.
  • From U8, someone goes in goal. Decide up front how you'll share the keeper role so it doesn't fall on the same child every week.
  • The FA wants children trying different positions. Its coaching tips include making “in-game decisions to help players develop and learn new skills and playing positions”. Here's a simple way to rotate positions fairly.

Your league can add its own rules on top of The FA's, such as squad-size limits or a cap on how many minutes a child can play in one day at a tournament. Check your league handbook as well as this guide.

Quick answers

What format do U9s play in 2026/27?

Under FutureFit, U8s and U9s play 5v5 from the 2026/27 season.

What format do U7s play under FutureFit?

U7s play 3v3, with no goalkeepers, no substitutes (everyone plays) and a facilitator instead of a referee. The FA guide gives a match length of 6–10 minutes.

When does 11v11 start under FutureFit?

From the 2026/27 season, 11v11 starts at U14. U12s and U13s play 9v9.

How long is a youth football match in 2026/27?

The FA's guide lists 6–10 minutes at U7 (3v3), 40 minutes at U8–U9 (5v5), 50 minutes at U10–U11 (7v7), 60 minutes at U12–U13 (9v9) and 70–90 minutes at U14 and above (11v11).

Can U11s head the ball?

No. In The FA's FutureFit laws table, heading is not part of the game at U7 to U11. It comes in at U12–U13 (9v9).

How much playing time should each child get?

The FA's FutureFit guide says that for U8 and above, equal playing time (at least 50%) is the best practice recommendation. At U7, all children play and there are no subs.
