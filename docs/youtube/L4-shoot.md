# L4 — Shoot list (actions only)

Just the taps, in the order you record them. **Don't speak while filming.**
The words are in `L4-voiceover.md`, recorded afterwards over the footage.
On-screen text and blurs are in `L4-edit.md`.

Each clip has a **Start on** and a **Stop when**. Do the taps slowly, about
twice as slow as feels natural, and **hold 2 seconds** at the start and end of
every clip. The edit can cut dead air, but it can't add it.

Record the whole list twice. Use the better take.

---

**Once per shoot day**

## Part A — creating a tournament

### Clip 1 · New tournament: the basics
- **Start on:** landing screen
- Tap **Tournament Mode** → **+ New Tournament**
- Tournament Name: `Autumn Cup`
- Date: today
- Size **5v5**, formation **1-2-1**
- **Stop when:** formation is picked

### Clip 2 · Duration, matches, half-time
- **Match Duration (mins)**: leave it on `10`, hold 2s
- **No. of group matches**: `6`
- **Matches have a half-time break**: hold on it 2s, **leave it off**
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 3 · Max subs
- Tap **Max substitutions per match** → hold on the options 2s → pick one
- Hold 4 seconds on the help text underneath
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 4 · The other settings, quickly
- Scroll past the fairness slider (leave on Equal), Position Rotation,
  **Rotate keeper?**. Don't change anything.
- Tap **Next: Select Players →**
- **Stop when:** the player list appears

### Clip 5 · Position chips
- **Start on:** the player list
- Hold 2s on the four chips next to a player
- Tap **DEF** off on one defender (e.g. Richard)
- **Stop when:** held 2s on the changed chips

### Clip 6 · Temporary player
- Tap **+ Add temporary player**
- Name `Leo` → tick **ATT** and **MID** → best **ATT** → skill **4** → save
- **Stop when:** Leo is in the list with the **Guest** tag, held 3s

### Clip 7 · Generate
- Tap **Generate Matches ▶**
- Let the generating screen run
- **Stop when:** the lobby has been up 2s

> **Off camera:** back out to the tournament list and open the **pre-built**
> Autumn Cup.

## Part B — on the pre-built tournament

### Clip 8 · The lobby
- **Start on:** the lobby, top of the page
- Scroll slowly down all 6 matches, and back up
- **Stop when:** back at the top, held 3s
- *(This is also the cold open.)*

### Clip 9 · Review all plans
- Tap **📋 Review all plans**
- Scroll slowly through every card, top to bottom
- **Stop when:** you reach the last card

### Clip 10 · A flagged card
- Scroll to a card with a *"things to know"* flag
- Hold 4 seconds (zoom in the edit)
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 11 · Share the day
- Tap **Share** in the header
- Hold on the stacked team-sheet image, scroll it slowly
- Close it without sending
- **Stop when:** back on Review all plans

### Clip 12 · Open a match
- Back to the lobby → tap **Open ▶** on match 1
- **Stop when:** the match has loaded, held 3s

### Clip 13 · Rename an opponent
- Back to the lobby → tap the **✎** next to "vs TBD" on match 1
- Type `Lions FC` → save
- **Stop when:** the new name shows in the list

### Clip 14 · Knockout match
- Tap **+ Add Knockout Match**
- Hold 2s on the **Knockout Competitiveness** slider
- Slide it to the right, hold 2s
- Tap **Cancel** (or **Generate ▶** if you want a 7th match in the stats)
- **Stop when:** back on the lobby

### Clip 15 · Stats and export
- Tap **📊 Tournament Stats** → scroll slowly
- Tap **⬆ Export spreadsheet**. Don't open the file.
- **Stop when:** held 2s after the export

### Clip 16 · Edit-tournament pencil
- Back to the lobby, top of the page
- Hold on the **✎** next to the tournament name for 3s. **Don't tap it.**
- **Stop when:** held
