# L3 — Voiceover (words only)

Record this **after** the footage is cut together, watching it as you read.
Each block is labelled with the clip it plays over (clip numbers from
`L3-shoot.md`).

This is the one coaches rewatch on a touchline. Every line has to survive being
half-heard in the rain, so say it plainly and don't rush.

Keep it chatty, like you're talking to another coach on the touchline. If a
line feels stiff when you say it, say it your way.

- Read at a normal pace. Every `·` is a breath.
- Fluffed a line? Pause, then say the **whole paragraph** again. Don't stop
  the recording. Cut the bad one in the edit.
- Record each block as its own file if that's easier.

Word count ≈ 900 → about 5½ minutes.

## 3 · The thing everybody gets wrong — over clips 3 to 6

**Don't cut this for time. It's the most important minute in the series.**

**3a · over clip 3 (tapping Next)**

> OK, this is the big one. · Next and Previous. They do **not** move the match
> on. They just change what you're looking at. ·
>
> Watch. I'll tap Next a few times… and the LIVE badge stays on the first
> quarter. · I've just had a peek at what's coming. That's on purpose. You want
> to see who's on next without actually doing anything.

**3b · over clip 4 (the prompt → Browse only)**

> Land on the start of a quarter that hasn't kicked off yet, and Level asks:
> Start this quarter? · Two options. · Browse only means "just looking". The
> match stays exactly where it was.

**3c · over clip 5 (Back to live)**

> If you ever lose track, tap Back to live. It takes you to whatever's actually
> being played right now.

**3d · over clip 6 (Start quarter)**

> And Start quarter is the real thing. That moves the match on and resets the
> clock for the period. ·
>
> That button's the *only* way the match moves on. Next will never do it by
> accident. Promise.

## 4 · Goals — over clips 7 to 9

**4a · over clip 7**

> Goals, then. · Press and hold the player for about a second. Don't just tap
> them. · Record Goal. Buzz, confetti, done. And they get a little badge with
> their tally.

**4b · over clip 8**

> Got the wrong kid? We've all done it. Tap the badge to take it off. · So: hold
> to add, tap the badge to take away. The tip at the top says that too, but it
> vanishes after a few seconds.

**4c · over clip 9**

> Works on the bench too, for the goal that goes in while you're halfway through
> a sub. ·
>
> One rule. Goals go on the quarter that's actually being played. If you've
> flicked ahead to the third quarter, you can't put a goal there. Keeps the match
> report honest.

## 5 · Someone goes off — over clips 10 and 11

**5a · over clip 10**

> Injury, or a kid who's just had enough. · Isaac's gone over on his ankle. Hold
> him. Remove from match. ·
>
> Here's the good bit. Level redoes the rest of the match without him, from this
> quarter on. You don't have to re-plan anything. · And it shows you the playing
> time impact first, so you can see who's picking up the extra minutes before
> you say yes.

**5b · over clip 11**

> He sits on the bench marked Removed. · Ten minutes later he's running about
> again and wants back on? Tap him, confirm, and Level fits him back in, and
> rebalances what's left. ·
>
> So it works both ways. You're never stuck with a plan that's stopped matching
> what's actually happening.

**5c · only if your take shows someone else covering goal after Isaac goes off**

> And because Isaac was one of my backup keepers, Level works out who covers goal
> from here too.
