# L3 — Shoot list (actions only)

Just the taps, in the order you record them. **Don't speak while filming.**
The words are in `L3-voiceover.md`, recorded afterwards over the footage.
On-screen text and blurs are in `L3-edit.md`.

Each clip has a **Start on** and a **Stop when**. Do the taps slowly, about
twice as slow as feels natural, and **hold 2 seconds** at the start and end of
every clip. The edit can cut dead air, but it can't add it.

Record the whole list twice. Use the better take.

⚠️ **Starting a match is a real state change.** If you fluff clips 1–6, tap
**← Return to plan review** (only works before period 2) or move on to a fresh
match. That's why you queue several.

---

## Before you start

**Once per shoot day**
- [ ] DND on, battery >80%, portrait, orientation lock on
- [ ] Signed in as the demo account, on **Richmond Greyhounds** (squad from
      `demo-squad.md`)
- [ ] **Two or three planned, not started** matches vs **Hillside Colts**
      (5v5, 1-2-1, 4 × 10 min), so a fluffed take means moving on, not rebuilding
- [ ] One **already finished** match, for the Full Time footage (clips 12–16).
      Don't try to play a whole match on camera.
- [ ] One extra planned match you can **Start** and then **End Match** early, for
      the early-end confirm in clip 12
- [ ] Decide: film at a real pitch? A touchline background sells this video more
      than any other. Screen capture gets cut in over it.

**Before every take**
- [ ] Check the planned match you're about to use hasn't been started yet
- [ ] 🔴 No real names on screen

## Part A — starting the match

### Clip 1 · Start Match
- **Start on:** match list, a planned (not started) Hillside Colts match
- Tap the match → the plan review screen
- Tap **Start Match**
- Hold on the pitch 5s: the clock counting up, the **● LIVE** badge, the goal tip at the top
- **Stop when:** the goal tip has disappeared on its own

### Clip 2 · Return to plan review
- **Start on:** the live pitch, Q1
- Hold 3s on **← Return to plan review**. Don't tap it.
- **Stop when:** held

---

## Part B — Next is not Start

### Clip 3 · Browsing ahead
- **Start on:** the live pitch, Q1
- Tap **Next ▶** three times, slowly, pausing 2s each
- Make sure the **● LIVE** badge is in shot each time (it stays on Q1)
- **Stop when:** you're on the 3rd tap

### Clip 4 · Browse only
- Keep tapping **Next ▶** until you land on the start of Q2
- The **"Start this quarter?"** prompt appears. Hold 3s on both buttons.
- Tap **Browse only**
- **Stop when:** the prompt has closed and you've held 2s

### Clip 5 · Back to live
- Tap **● Back to live**
- **Stop when:** you're back on the live quarter, held 2s

### Clip 6 · Start quarter
- Tap **Next ▶** until the **"Start this quarter?"** prompt appears again
- Tap **Start quarter**
- **Stop when:** the period clock has reset and you've held 3s

## Part C — goals and subs

### Clip 7 · Record a goal
- **Start on:** the live pitch
- **Press and hold** Jamie (**#9**) for about a second → the action menu opens
- Tap **⚽ Record Goal** → confetti
- Hold 3s on the goal badge on his token
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 8 · Undo a goal
- Tap the goal badge on Jamie
- **Stop when:** the badge is gone, held 2s

### Clip 9 · Bench player
- **Press and hold** a bench player → the action menu opens. Hold 3s.
- Close the menu without recording anything
- **Stop when:** menu closed

### Clip 10 · Remove a player
- **Press and hold** Isaac (**#5**) → **Remove from match**
- Hold on "Regenerating plan…" and then the **Playing time impact** panel, 4s
- Tap **Apply anyway**
- **Stop when:** Isaac shows on the bench tagged **Removed**, held 3s

> **Off camera:** step through the rest of the plan. Does someone else now
> cover a goal period that Isaac had? Note it. The voiceover has a line that's
> only true if they do.

### Clip 11 · Reinstate
- Tap Isaac on the bench → the confirm → **Reinstate player**
- **Stop when:** he's back in the plan, held 3s

## Part D — full time (use the finished match, and the spare)

### Clip 12 · End Match early
- **Start on:** the spare match, started and live, early in the match
- Tap **End Match** → the "are you sure" confirm appears. Hold 3s.
- **Stop when:** held (confirm or cancel, either is fine)

### Clip 13 · Full Time card
- **Start on:** the finished match's **Full Time** card
- Type the opposition's goals
- Hold 3s on the score, the badge and the scorers
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 14 · Hide score
- Toggle **Hide score**. The scoreline changes to **X–X**.
- Hold 3s
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 15 · Share Result
- Tap **Share Result**
- Hold on the generated image 3s (the scoreline should still show X–X)
- Close the share sheet. **Don't send it anywhere.**
- **Stop when:** back on the Full Time card

### Clip 16 · Reopening a finished match
- Back to the match list. Hold 2s on the finished match's **FT** badge.
- Tap it → lands on the Full Time card
- Hold 2s on **View on pitch** (don't tap it)
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 17 · Cold open (filmed last, goes first)
- Any live match: the pitch with **● LIVE** showing and the clock running, held 5s
- **Stop when:** held
