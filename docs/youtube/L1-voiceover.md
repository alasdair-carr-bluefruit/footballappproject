## 7 · Adding a player — over clip 8

**7a · over Squad Management → Add Player → typing the name**

> Level points you straight at your squad, which is right. Nothing else works
> until your players are in. · Squad Management, then Add Player. ·
>
> Quick tip: if you've got a big squad, this bit's easier on a laptop. Level
> works in any browser, not just on your phone. Sign in with the same email and
> everything's there. ·
>
> Name first. First names are fine. That's what you'll shout across the pitch
> anyway. The only rule is that names have to be unique, and I'll show you what
> happens when they're not.

**7b · over ticking DEF and MID, and the FA note**

> Now "Can Play". This is the bit worth understanding, because it's what makes
> the rotations good. · Four options: goalkeeper, defence, midfield, and attack. ·
>
> Tick every position this player's genuinely happy playing. Leave one unticked,
> and you're telling Level to keep them out of it. · And read the note
> underneath: the FA discourages fixing children into one position before
> they're twelve.

**7c · over Best Position → MID**

> Two warnings. · If you tick GK and nothing else, that player will never be
> given an outfield position. Not once, all match. That's right for a proper
> keeper. It's not right for a ten-year-old who fancies a go in goal. For them,
> tick GK *and* their outfield positions. ·
>
> And if you leave DEF unticked, that's a hard rule. Level won't put them at the
> back. The rest are best-effort: if a slot can't be filled any other way, Level
> uses someone out of position, and tells you before kick-off. ·
>
> Best Position is their strongest. Level leans towards it, without parking them
> there all match.

**7d · over shirt number and skill → Save**

> Shirt number's optional, but put them in. They show on the pitch instead of
> initials, which is much easier to read from the touchline. If two players
> share a number, the second one goes red. ·
>
> Skill, one to five. Nobody but you ever sees it. It's so Level can spread your
> stronger and weaker players across the match, instead of putting your best
> five on at once. Most players are threes. Be honest, and move on quickly. ·
>
> Save.
