# Intro and outro — shared across L1–L4

The same opening and closing on every video, so the series feels like a set.
Each script's COLD OPEN and wrap point back here.

## The running order

```
COLD OPEN (hook, 10–15s)  →  STING (2–3s)  →  [L1 only: who I am, ~15s]  →  BEAT 1 …
… wrap  →  END CARD (sting reversed or held, 10–20s)
```

**The hook comes before the sting.** Most people decide in the first few
seconds whether to keep watching. So open on the problem or the payoff, and
then roll the logo. A logo first gives them nothing to stay for. Each script's
cold open is already written to be that hook.

---

## The sting (every video)

**Clip:** the Level circle rolling in. Use the same file, same length and same
sound every time.

- **Length:** keep it under 3 seconds. Trim it if the clip runs longer. The
  strategy doc's rule is intros under 8 seconds *in total*, and the cold open
  already uses most of that.
- **Title on the last second:** while the circle settles, fade in the episode
  title in Space Mono, Signal Lime on Studio Green (the brand pair), e.g.

  ```
  LEVEL  ·  PART 1 OF 4
  Getting started
  ```

  | Video | Title line |
  |---|---|
  | L1 | `Getting started` |
  | L2 | `Planning a match` |
  | L3 | `Matchday` |
  | L4 | `Tournament day` |

- **Sound:** a short whoosh or click as the circle lands, then silence. No music
  under the narration. It fights with the voiceover on phone speakers.
- **No narration over the sting.** Let it breathe and start Beat 1 on the cut.

---

## L1 only — who I am (~15s, after the sting)

The first video is where people decide whether to trust you. Say once, briefly,
why Level exists. The other three skip this and go straight into Beat 1.

**Screen:** you, to camera, ideally at a pitch. Failing that, the landing screen
on the phone in your hand. Keep it under 15 seconds.

**Narration (draft — make it yours):**
> "I'm Ali. I [coach an under-[X]s side / have been coaching grassroots for [X]
> seasons], and I built Level because I was fed up of doing rotations on the
> back of a team sheet and still getting them wrong. It's free, it's built for
> coaches like us, and this series shows you everything in about twenty minutes."

Fill in the brackets with your real details. Don't name your actual club or
any children.

---

## End card (every video)

YouTube's end screen (the clickable "next video" and "subscribe" boxes) can
only sit in the **last 5–20 seconds** and needs footage under it. Plan for it:

- **Last 15 seconds:** the sting again, either reversed (circle rolls *out*) or
  the settled logo held on Studio Green, with `keepthingslevel.com` underneath.
- Leave the **left half clear** for YouTube's end-screen boxes:
  - L1–L3: link the next video in the series
  - L4: link a playlist of all four
- Keep the wrap's last line ("Next: …") just *before* the end card starts, so
  the next-video box arrives as you say it.
- Put the feedback line on screen here too: `Ideas? hello@keepthingslevel.com`

---

## Timings

The beat timings and chapter timestamps in each script **don't include the sting
or the L1 intro**. After the edit, push every chapter after `0:00` back by the
sting length (plus ~15s on L1), and check them against the final cut before
publishing. YouTube rejects a chapter list if the first one isn't `0:00`.
