# L3 — "Matchday: kick-off to full time"

**Target length:** 5:00 · **Format:** portrait phone capture, voiceover recorded separately
**Working title (YouTube):** *Running matchday with Level — subs, goals and full time*

The one coaches will rewatch on a touchline. Every instruction must survive being
half-heard in the rain.

---

## Pre-flight checklist

- [ ] A **planned, not started** match ready on Demo Rovers (7v7, 4 quarters)
- [ ] A second, already-**finished** match for the Full Time / share footage —
      you don't want to fake a whole match on camera
- [ ] Do Not Disturb on, portrait lock, >80% battery
- [ ] 🔴 No real names on screen
- [ ] Decide whether you're filming this at an actual pitch — a real touchline
      background sells this video more than any other. Screen capture can be
      cut in over it.

**One-take warning:** starting a match is a real state change. If you fluff it,
use "← Return to plan review" (only works before period 2) or make a fresh match.
Have two or three planned matches queued so you can just move on.

## BEAT 1 — Start Match — 0:12 to 0:55

**Screen:** Open the planned match → review screen → **Start Match**. Show the
clock start, the ● LIVE badge, and the goal tip appearing.

**Narration:**
> "You're at the pitch, the plan's good, so: Start Match.
>
> Three things just happened. The clock started — that's a count-up, real elapsed
> time. The match is marked live. And that tip along the top tells you how to
> record a goal, which we'll come to.
>
> That clock is tougher than it looks. It's stored against this match, so you can
> back out to your match list, lock your phone, even reload the page, and it'll
> still show the right time. It doesn't reset until the match ends.
>
> And if you jumped the gun — Return to plan review puts it back. That only works
> before you start the second period, though. After that, you're committed."

**On-screen text:**
- `The clock survives reloads and backing out`
- `"← Return to plan review" — only before period 2`

---

## BEAT 2 — The thing everybody gets wrong — 0:55 to 1:55

⚠️ **The single most important 60 seconds in the whole series.**

**Screen:** Tap **Next ▶** a few times, browsing ahead. Point out that the ● LIVE
badge stays put on Q1. Land on the start of Q2 and let the **"Start this
quarter?"** prompt appear. Show both buttons. Tap **Browse only** first. Then use
**● Back to live** to return. Then go forward again and tap **Start quarter**.

**Narration:**
> "Right. Next and Previous. These do **not** move the match on. They just move
> what you're looking at.
>
> Watch — I'll tap Next a few times, and see the LIVE badge stays on the first
> quarter. All I've done is have a look at what's coming. That's deliberate: you
> want to see who's on next without committing to anything.
>
> When you land on the start of a quarter that hasn't started yet, Level asks:
> Start this quarter? Two answers. Browse only leaves the match exactly where it
> was. Start quarter is the real thing — that moves the match on and resets the
> period clock.
>
> So if you're ever lost, tap Back to live and it takes you to the quarter that's
> actually being played. And that prompt is the *only* way the match progresses.
> Next never does it by accident."

**On-screen text (hold both, 4s each):**
- `Next ▶ = look ahead. It does NOT start the quarter.`
- `"Start this quarter?" → Start quarter = the real thing`

---

## BEAT 3 — Goals — 1:55 to 2:45

**Screen:** Press and hold a player on the pitch. The action menu opens.
**⚽ Record Goal**. Confetti. Show the goal badge on the player. Then tap the
badge to undo. Then hold a **bench** player to show it works there too.

**Narration:**
> "Goals. Hold a player — press and hold, about a second, don't just tap — and you
> get Record Goal. Buzz, confetti, done, and they pick up a badge showing their
> tally.
>
> Wrong player? Tap the badge to take one off. The tip at the top said exactly
> that, and it disappears after a few seconds, so: hold to add, tap the badge to
> remove.
>
> Works on bench players too, for the goal that goes in while you're mid-change.
>
> One rule: goals go on the quarter that's actually live. If you've browsed
> forward to look at the third quarter, you can't record a goal there — Level
> keeps them on the live period so your match report stays honest."

**On-screen text:**
- `Hold to add · tap the badge to undo`
- `Goals record on the LIVE quarter only`

---

## BEAT 4 — Someone goes off — 2:45 to 3:40

**Screen:** Hold a player → **Remove from match**. Show "Regenerating plan…", the
**Playing time impact** panel, tap **Apply anyway**. Show them on the bench tagged
**Removed**. Tap them → reinstate.

**Narration:**
> "Injury, or a lad who's had enough. Hold them, Remove from match.
>
> Now here's the useful bit. Level rebuilds the rest of the match without them,
> from this quarter on — you don't re-plan anything. And it shows you the playing
> time impact first, so you know who's about to pick up the extra minutes before
> you accept it.
>
> They sit on the bench marked Removed. If they shake it off and want back on, tap
> them, confirm, and Level folds them back into the remaining quarters — again,
> rebalancing what's left.
>
> Both directions are non-destructive. You're never stuck with a plan that's
> stopped matching reality."

**On-screen text:** `Removing a player re-plans the rest of the match`

---

## BEAT 5 — Full Time — 3:40 to 4:40

**Screen:** Cut to the pre-finished match. **End Match** → the Full Time card.
Type opponent goals. Show scorers. Toggle **Hide score** — the scoreline goes to
X–X. Tap **Share Result** and show the generated image.

**Narration:**
> "Last quarter played, End Match. If you end it early — someone's called it off,
> or the light's gone — it'll check you meant it.
>
> Full Time. Put the opposition's goals in, and there's your card: the score, your
> badge, and your scorers.
>
> Now, Hide score. FA guidance says don't publish results at under-11 and below,
> so this masks the scoreline to X–X — on screen *and* in the image you share —
> while keeping the scorers, because the kids who scored still want their name up.
> Level remembers it for this match, so if you come back next week to share it
> again, it's still hidden.
>
> Share Result gives you the image straight into WhatsApp on a phone, or downloads
> it on a laptop. Save Image if you just want to keep it."

**On-screen text:**
- `Hide score — FA guidance, U11 and below`
- `Scorers stay visible`

---

## BEAT 6 — Wrap — 4:40 to 5:00

**Screen:** Match list showing the finished match with its **FT** badge. Tap it —
lands back on the Full Time card.

**Narration:**
> "The match saves with the result on your list, and tapping it takes you straight
> back to this card rather than the pitch — because reopening a finished match is
> nearly always about sharing it. View on pitch is there if you want to look at
> who played where.
>
> If you do need to correct a goal afterwards, Level checks first — a finished
> report shouldn't change by accident.
>
> That's matchday. Next one: tournaments, where it balances minutes across a whole
> day of matches."

**On-screen text:** `Next: tournament day`
