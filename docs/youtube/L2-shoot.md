# L2 — Shoot list (actions only)

Just the taps, in the order you record them. **Don't speak while filming.**
The words are in `L2-voiceover.md`, recorded afterwards over the footage.
On-screen text and blurs are in `L2-edit.md`.

Each clip has a **Start on** and a **Stop when**. Do the taps slowly, about
twice as slow as feels natural, and **hold 2 seconds** at the start and end of
every clip.

Record the whole list twice. Use the better take.

---

## Before you start

**Once per shoot day**
- [ ] DND on, battery >80%, portrait, orientation lock on
- [ ] Signed in as the demo account. **Richmond Greyhounds** has all 10 from
      `demo-squad.md`, with the badge and shirt numbers set (both show on the
      shared team sheet).
- [ ] **Dry run:** make a throwaway match (Westfield Juniors, 5v5, 1-2-1,
      untick Sam B + Richard), generate, and **write down the banner's exact
      lines**. You'll read them in the voiceover. Usually it's a 2-slot
      game-time gap (Thierry) and *"Jamie plays MID … only picked ATT"*, but
      the plan changes every time you generate. Delete the throwaway match.
- [ ] Don't tap **Start Match** at any point. You finish on a plan, not a match.

**Before every take**
- [ ] No leftover Westfield match from the last take (delete it)
- [ ] No real names, no real email, no `?login=` in the URL bar

## Part A — the match settings

### Clip 1 · New match
- **Start on:** landing screen
- Tap **Season Mode** → **+ New Match**
- Opponent: `Westfield Juniors`
- Set the date
- Tap **✈️ Away**
- Hold 2s on Team size **5v5** and formation **1-2-1**
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 2 · Structure flips
- Tap **9v9** → hold 3s on Match Structure showing **2 Halves**
- Tap **5v5** → hold 3s on **4 Quarters × 10 min** and **1-2-1**
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 3 · Playing Time slider
- Slide **Playing Time** slowly from 0 to about 70
- Hold 5s on the two warning lines and the FA line underneath
- Slide back to **0** (Equal)
- **Stop when:** it's on Equal, held 2s

### Clip 4 · Rotation and keeper
- Tap each **Position Rotation** option slowly: **Fixed**, **Flexible**,
  **All-rounder**. Land on **All-rounder**.
- Tap the **(?)** next to **Rotate keeper?** → hold 4s on the explanation
- Leave the switch **on**
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 5 · Who's turned up
- Tap **Next: Select Players →**
- Hold 2s on the list, everyone ticked
- Untick **Sam B**, then **Richard**
- Tap **Generate Rotation ▶**
- **Stop when:** the Review the plan screen has loaded

## Part B — reading the plan

### Clip 6 · The grid
- **Start on:** Review the plan
- Hold 3s wide
- Zoom (or scroll slowly) across the grid row by row, including the ⚡ row
  at the bottom
- **Stop when:** you've covered all the columns

### Clip 7 · Slots per player
- Scroll to the **Slots per player** strip, hold 4s
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 8 · The calm banner
- Scroll to the banner: *"Worth knowing before kick-off"*, no ⚠
- Hold 6s so every line is readable
- If a line says someone's out of position, scroll back to the grid and hold
  3s on that **dashed** cell
- **Stop when:** held
- ⚠️ Film this **before** any tinkering. After you edit a slot, the banner
  changes for good.

---

## Part C — tinkering

### Clip 9 · On the pitch
- Tap **⚽ View on pitch**
- Tap **Next** twice, slowly, holding 2s on each slot
- **Stop when:** on the third slot

### Clip 10 · Tinker: drag
- Tap **Tinker** → hold 3s on the lighter pitch and the **Tinkering** badge
- Drag one player onto another → they swap
- Hold 2s on the **LOCKED** badge
- **Stop when:** held

### Clip 11 · Tinker: tap
- Tap a different player → the **Swap player** list opens
- Hold 4s (it shows each option's slot count)
- Pick someone
- **Stop when:** the swap has happened

### Clip 12 · Recalculate (cancel it)
- Tap **↻ Recalculate rest of match** → confirm box appears
- Hold 5s
- **Cancel**
- **Stop when:** back on the pitch

### Clip 13 · Done
- Tap **Done**
- Hold on the **"Sorted. Plan updated."** toast
- **Stop when:** the toast has gone

### Clip 14 · The firmer banner
- Go back to **Review the plan**
- Scroll to the banner: *"⚠ Worth a look before kick-off"* and the FA line
- Hold 6s
- **Stop when:** held
- No banner? Your edit didn't leave anything to flag. Go back to the pitch,
  Tinker, put **Jamie** in a midfield spot in another slot, Done, try again.

## Part D — share and finish

### Clip 15 · Team sheet
- **Start on:** Review the plan
- Tap **Share** (top right)
- In the share sheet, pick **WhatsApp**, but **don't send**. (Or, if the sheet
  shows a preview, hold on that instead.)
- Hold 3s on the image: badge, date · vs Westfield Juniors · Away, the grid,
  the **BENCH** row, the ⚡ row
- Back out of WhatsApp without sending
- **Stop when:** back on Review the plan

### Clip 16 · Settled plan
- Hold on Review the plan
- Scroll to **Start Match**. Hover near it. **Don't tap it.**
- **Stop when:** held 3s

### Clip 17 · Cold open (filmed last, goes first)
- Wide shot of the finished plan grid, hold 4s
- Back out to the season list → **+ New Match** → hold 2s on the blank form
- **Stop when:** held
