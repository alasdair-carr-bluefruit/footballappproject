# YouTube Tutorial Strategy — Level

> Working doc. Owner: Ali. Created 2026-09-11, at ~8 beta coaches.
> Purpose: a shootable plan for a small library of tutorial videos that (a) gets a
> new coach from invite to a generated plan without hand-holding, and (b) doubles
> as top-of-funnel content for `keepthingslevel.com`.

---

## 1. What these videos are for

Two jobs, and they pull in different directions — keep them in separate formats:

| Job | Format | Success measure |
|---|---|---|
| **Onboard** an invited coach so you stop doing it over WhatsApp | Long-form, linked from the app + invite email | Beta coaches reach "plan generated" unaided; fewer support messages |
| **Attract** coaches who've never heard of Level | Shorts, searchable titles, one idea each | Views → site visits → early-access requests |

Rule of thumb: **longs teach a flow, shorts answer a question.** Never make a
short that only makes sense if you watched a long.

**Audience reality check.** Grassroots volunteer coaches. Watching on a phone,
one-handed, probably the night before a match, possibly in a car park. So:
portrait or square where possible, big on-screen text, no jargon, and say the
useful thing in the first 5 seconds.

### L1 — Getting started: your invite, your squad (5:00)

**Open on:** "You've got an invite link to Level. Three minutes from now you'll
have your squad in and be ready to plan a match."

**Beats**
1. **The invite** (0:00–1:00) — tap the link, enter email + name, you're in.
   Level is invite-only right now; one invite = one coach, and it expires.
2. **Signing in later** (1:00–1:45) — no password. Enter email, get a link,
   **tap the Confirm button on the page** — that's the bit people miss.
3. **Name your team + badge** (1:45–2:30) — the badge appears on the shareable
   full-time card, so it's worth 30 seconds now.
4. **Add players** (2:30–4:30) — name, the four "Can Play" checkboxes
   (**GK / DEF / MID / ATT** — forwards are labelled ATT on screen), Best
   Position, optional shirt number, skill 1–5.
5. **Wrap** (4:30–5:00) — "Squad's in. Next video: your first rotation."

**Gotchas to say out loud**
- 🔴 **Tap "Confirm" on the sign-in page.** The link never signs you in just by
  loading — that's deliberate, because corporate mail scanners pre-open links and
  would burn your one-time token. If you land on the login screen saying the link
  expired, just request a fresh one.
- 🔴 **Open the link in your normal browser.** If you tap it inside WhatsApp,
  Facebook, or Outlook, those apps use their own private cookie jar and the
  session may not carry over. Level shows a nudge with a **Copy link** button —
  paste it into Safari/Chrome.
- 🟠 **Ticking only GK means they never play outfield** — not once, all match.
  That's usually not what you want for a 10-year-old who quite likes going in
  goal; tick GK *and* their outfield positions for them.
- 🟠 **Unticking DEF is a hard rule**; the other position picks are best-effort.
  Level tries hard to keep players in their picks, but if a slot can't be filled
  any other way it will use someone out of position — and then tell you about it
  on the review screen.
- 🟠 **Player names must be unique** within a squad. Two Jacks → "Jack S" and
  "Jack T".
- 🟠 **Deleting a player deletes their history** — goals and past match data go
  with them. The confirm dialog spells this out ("All previous match data
  including goals scored will be lost"); read it rather than tapping through.
- ⚪ Duplicate shirt number → the second one shows red. Not fatal, just a heads-up.
- ⚪ If your team is already set up on the server, the tutorial screen is skipped
  when you sign in on a new phone. That's expected, not a bug.

### L2 — Planning a match: generate, review, tinker (6:00)

**Open on:** "This is the bit that used to take me twenty minutes on the back of
an envelope."

**Beats**
1. **New match settings** (0:00–2:00) — date, opponent, home/away, team size,
   formation, minutes per period, show timer, **Rotate keeper?**, the fairness
   slider, rotation intensity.
2. **Who's available** (2:00–2:30) — everyone's ticked by default; untick the
   ones who are away. This is *the* input that matters most.
3. **Generate → the review screen** (2:30–4:00) — walk the grid: one row per
   player, one column per slot, the counts strip at the bottom. Then read the
   warning banner properly, line by line.
4. **Tinkering** (4:00–5:30) — **⚽ View on pitch** → **Tinker** → drag a player
   onto another, or tap a coin to open the **Swap player** picker (it shows how
   many slots each option has). Slot goes LOCKED. **Done** → "Sorted. Plan
   updated."
5. **Wrap** (5:30–6:00) — "Plan's ready. Don't tap Start Match until you're at
   the pitch — next video."

**Gotchas to say out loud**
- 🔴 **"↻ Recalculate rest of match" wipes your edits in later slots.** It reflows
  everything *after* the slot you're viewing; the current slot and everything
  before it stay exactly as you left them. Use it after a big change, not as a
  general refresh.
- 🔴 **The warnings are information, not errors.** A gap in game time on a
  competitive setting is shown in a calmer tone because you asked for it. Nothing
  ever blocks you from starting.
- 🟠 **Picking 9v9 defaults you to 2 Halves**, not 4 Quarters — fewer, longer
  slots, so each substitution decision matters more. It's only a default: the
  **Match Structure** picker is right there if you want to override it. Minutes
  cap at 45 for a half and 22.5 for a quarter.
- 🟠 **"Rotate keeper?" is on by default and matters most in small squads.** With
  a dedicated keeper (GK ticked alone) and it turned off, they're in goal every slot and play
  roughly double everyone else. It's forced off automatically if you don't have
  enough players to cover goal from the bench.
- 🟠 **Prev/Next are disabled while you're tinkering.** Tap Done first. This
  confuses people every time.
- 🟠 **"Manually create plan" turns the fair-rotation engine off.** You get a
  blank plan and own every decision; it carries the previous slot's lineup
  forward as a starting point. There's an explainer overlay before you commit
  ("Manually create plan?" → **Create manual plan**) — don't skip it in the
  video, show it.
- 🟠 **You can re-edit a planned match** with the ✎ pencil on the match list — but
  only while it's still planned. Once it's live or finished, the settings freeze.
- ⚪ The sub cap per period is a *soft* preference. Full-team coverage and fair
  time win over it, so don't be alarmed by an extra change.
- ⚪ Push the fairness slider past 60 and you get two lines: the caution ("Not
  recommended for grassroots — some players will get noticeably less pitch
  time") and a light-hearted nudge under it ("Steady on, Pep…"). Read the
  caution out; the joke can just sit on screen.

### L3 — Matchday: kick-off to full time (5:00)

**Open on:** "Phone in one hand, whistle in the other. Here's the whole match."

**Beats**
1. **Start Match** (0:00–0:45) — locks the plan, starts the count-up clock, and a
   tip appears telling you how to record a goal.
2. **The single most important idea** (0:45–1:45) — **Next ▶ only moves the
   view.** The match progresses when you browse to the start of the next period
   and answer the **"Start this quarter?"** prompt with **Start quarter** (the
   other option is **Browse only**). Show the ● LIVE badge and the
   **● Back to live** button.
3. **Goals** (1:45–2:30) — hold a player (on pitch or bench) → **⚽ Record
   Goal**. Confetti, buzz, done. The on-screen tip says it all: "Hold a player to
   record a goal ⚽ · Tap the badge to undo" — show the undo.
4. **Someone goes off** (2:30–3:30) — hold → **Remove from match**. Level
   regenerates the rest of the plan and shows a **Playing time impact** panel
   (**Apply anyway** to accept). They sit on the bench tagged "Removed"; tap them
   to reinstate.
5. **Full Time** (3:30–4:45) — enter **Opponent goals**, the ⚽ Goal scorers
   list, the **Hide score** toggle, then **Share Result** (or **Save Image**).
6. **Wrap** (4:45–5:00).

**Gotchas to say out loud**
- 🔴 **Next ▶ is not "start the next quarter".** Browsing ahead never commits
  anything — the **"Start this quarter?"** prompt (Start quarter / Browse only)
  is the only thing that advances the live period and resets the period clock.
- 🔴 **The clock survives everything.** It's real elapsed time, stored per match,
  so backing out to the match list or reloading the page won't reset it. It only
  clears when the match ends.
- 🟠 **Hold, don't tap.** About 0.6s. Say it slowly; show the press. Undo a
  mis-tapped goal by tapping the player's goal badge.
- 🟠 **Periods already played are frozen** — the Tinker button disappears on them.
  You can still tinker the live period and any future one.
- 🟠 **"← Return to plan review" only works before you start period 2.** After
  that the match is genuinely under way.
- 🟠 **Hide score is there for a reason**: FA guidance says don't publish results
  at U11 and below. It masks the scoreline to X–X on screen *and* in the shared
  image, keeps the scorers, and remembers the setting per match.
- 🟠 **Ending early asks for confirmation** ("End match early? Not all slots have
  been played.") unless you've genuinely reached the final period.
- ⚪ On a phone the share button opens the native share sheet with the PNG
  attached; on a laptop it just downloads the image. Both fine.
- ⚪ Reopening a finished match lands on the Full Time card, not the pitch — tap
  "View on pitch" to browse the slots.

### L4 — Tournament day (6:00)

**Open on:** "Six matches, one afternoon, and every parent counting minutes.
This is the mode that saves the day."

**Beats**
1. **Why it's a separate mode** (0:00–0:45) — playing time is balanced **across
   the whole day**, not per match.
2. **Create the tournament** (0:45–2:15) — Tournament Name, date,
   size/formation, **Match Duration (mins)**, **No. of group matches**,
   **Matches have a half-time break**, **Max substitutions per match**, fairness,
   Position Rotation, Rotate keeper.
3. **Who's playing** (2:15–3:30) — tick availability, **tap the position chips**
   to override a player's positions just for today, and **+ Add temporary
   player** for anyone borrowed for the day. (Careful with vocabulary: the button
   says "temporary player", the squad list tags them **Guest**. Say both once,
   then pick one and stick to it.)
4. **Generate Matches ▶ + 📋 Review all plans** (3:30–4:30) — one stacked page,
   one grid per match, warnings per card, **Open ▶** into any of them.
5. **Running the day** (4:30–5:30) — rename opponents with ✎ as the draw firms
   up, **+ Add Knockout Match** (it gets its own **Knockout Competitiveness**
   slider), **📊 Tournament Stats**, **⬆ Export spreadsheet**.
6. **Wrap** (5:30–6:00).

**Gotchas to say out loud**
- 🔴 **Order matters.** Cross-match fairness works by accumulating what each
  player has already played, so generate and run the matches in order. "Review
  all plans" fills in any missing plans in match order for exactly this reason.
- 🔴 **Editing a tournament regenerates the planned matches.** Any tinkering you
  did on a not-yet-played match can be lost. Tinker *after* you've settled the
  setup, not before. (Matches that already have a plan are skipped by "Review all
  plans", so tinkered plans survive *that* path.)
- 🟠 **Position chips here are tournament-only overrides** — they don't change the
  player's squad profile.
- 🟠 **Temporary players are tournament-only too** — "This player is only
  available for this tournament." They don't join your squad or appear in season
  stats.
- 🟠 **You can only delete a match that hasn't started.**
- 🟠 **Level doesn't know your league's daily playing-time caps.** Some county
  leagues (Cornwall, for one) cap total minutes per child per day across a
  tournament. Level balances fairly between your players but won't enforce a
  county cap — that one's still on you.
- ⚪ Knockout matches get their own fairness slider, so you can be more
  competitive for the final without touching the group-stage plans.

## 4. Shorts — the format

**Template (30s):**

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | The question as on-screen text. "Why is my keeper playing every quarter?" |
| 0:03–0:20 | Screen recording of the fix. Your voice, no music bed under speech. |
| 0:20–0:27 | The one-line takeaway, repeated as text. |
| 0:27–0:30 | "Level — keepthingslevel.com" |

**Rules**
- Portrait 9:16, recorded on a real phone, real thumbs. Simulator footage looks fake.
- Caption everything. Most Shorts play muted.
- One idea. If you need "and also", it's two shorts.
- Title = the search query, not the feature name. "Fair sub rotation for kids
  football" beats "Level fairness slider overview".

**Priority order to shoot:** 7 (Rotate keeper) → 14 (Next doesn't start the
quarter) → 13 (record a goal) → 5 (fairness slider) → 2 (position checkboxes) →
11 (recalculate) → 16 (hide the score) → 9 (bench streak warning).

## 5. Master gotcha list

Everything above in one place, so nothing gets dropped when you cut a video short.
🔴 = will cause a support message if unsaid.

**Account & access**
- 🔴 Magic-link sign-in needs an explicit **Confirm** tap on the page.
- 🔴 In-app browsers (WhatsApp / Facebook / Outlook) can swallow the session — use Copy link → paste in Safari/Chrome.
- 🟠 Invite links are one-time and expire.
- 🟠 Changing your email sends a confirm link to the **new** address; the old one keeps working until you tap it, and a reclaim link goes to the old address in case it wasn't you.
- 🟠 Installed as an app and looking stale? **Settings → Check for updates** clears the cache and reloads.

**Squad**
- 🟠 GK ticked on its own = never plays outfield. Positions read **GK / DEF /
  MID / ATT** on screen.
- 🟠 Unticked DEF is hard; other position picks are best-effort.
- 🟠 Unique names required; deleting a player deletes their history.
- ⚪ Duplicate shirt number shows red.

**Planning**
- 🔴 "Recalculate rest of match" discards edits in later slots.
- 🔴 Warnings inform, they never block.
- 🟠 9v9 = halves; period-length caps 22.5 / 45.
- 🟠 "Rotate keeper?" default on; auto-off when the squad can't cover goal.
- 🟠 Prev/Next disabled while tinkering.
- 🟠 Manual mode disables fair rotation.
- 🟠 Planned matches editable via ✎; live/finished ones frozen.
- ⚪ Sub cap is a soft preference.

**Matchday**
- 🔴 Next ▶ moves the view; the "Start this quarter?" prompt advances the match.
- 🔴 The clock is real elapsed time and survives reloads.
- 🟠 Hold (~0.6s) for goals and removals; tap the badge to undo a goal.
- 🟠 Played periods are frozen.
- 🟠 "Return to plan review" only before period 2.
- 🟠 Hide score for U11 and below.

**Tournament**
- 🔴 Generate and play in order — fairness accumulates.
- 🔴 Editing the tournament regenerates planned matches.
- 🟠 Position overrides and temporary players are tournament-only.
- 🟠 League daily-minute caps aren't enforced.

## 6. Production notes

**Demo data — set this up once and reuse it.**
Create a separate team called something like **"Demo Rovers"** via the team
switcher, with 10–12 obviously-fictional players (Sam Archer, Kit Fletcher…) and
a placeholder badge. Then:
- 🔴 **Never show real children's names on screen.** Non-negotiable, and it also
  keeps the videos evergreen when your squad changes.
- 🔴 **Never show a magic link, invite link, or the URL bar containing
  `?login=` / `?invite=` / `?email_change=`.** Anyone who pauses the video has
  your session. For L1, either cut away at the tap or blur the address bar.
- Blur your email on the Settings screen.
- Delete the demo team when you're done, or keep it — it's isolated from your
  real squads either way.

**Capture**
- Phone: iOS Screen Recording, Do Not Disturb on, battery charged (a low-battery
  banner mid-take is a re-shoot).
- Voiceover separately after the fact, over a silent screen recording — much
  easier than narrating while tapping, and the pacing is better.
- Longs: record each beat as its own clip. A fluffed line costs you 40 seconds,
  not the whole take.

**Editing**
- Zoom in on small UI (the position chips, the warning banner). Assume a 6-inch
  phone held at arm's length.
- On-screen text for every gotcha, not just voiceover.
- Keep a consistent lower-third with the Level wordmark and the Signal Lime
  accent — brand tokens are in `BRAND.md` / `assets/brand/tokens.json`.

**Description template**
```
<One line: what this video gets you.>

Level is a free rotation planner for grassroots youth football coaches —
fair playing time, sorted before you get to the pitch.
👉 keepthingslevel.com

Chapters:
0:00 …

Level is invite-only during the beta — request access at keepthingslevel.com.
```

## 7. Publish plan

**Order:** L1 → shorts 1, 2, 7 → L2 → shorts 5, 6, 11 → L3 → shorts 13, 14, 16 →
L4 → the rest.

**Cadence:** one long a week, two shorts between each. Front-load L1 — it's the
one that immediately pays for itself with the 8 coaches you already have.

**Wire them in.** A tutorial nobody finds is wasted:
- Link L1 from the invite email and from the app's landing screen.
- Embed L1 and L2 on `keepthingslevel.com`; add a `/tutorials` page (this also
  feeds the T2.2 SEO work already on the roadmap).
- One playlist, "Getting started with Level", ordered L1→L4.
- Pin the shorts that answer the questions your beta coaches actually ask —
  update this list once you've collected a few weeks of bug reports.

**Measure:** requests for early access after each publish, and whether L1's
audience-retention graph has a cliff. A cliff in the sign-in section means the
magic-link flow needs a product fix, not a better video.

## 9. Appendix — say what's on the screen

Everything in this doc was swept against `frontend/index.html` on 2026-09-11.
The trap when writing about your own app is reaching for the vocabulary in the
codebase rather than the words a coach actually sees. Corrected instances:

| Don't say (internal) | Say (on screen) |
|---|---|
| Specialist / preferred / can-play / emergency GK tiers | Nothing — the coach just ticks **Can Play** boxes. Describe the *behaviour*: "GK ticked alone → never plays outfield" |
| FWD | **ATT** |
| Specialist / Balanced / All-rounder | **Fixed / Flexible / All-rounder** |
| Guest player | **+ Add temporary player** (though the list tags them "Guest") |
| Jump to live | **● Back to live** |
| "Just browsing" | **Browse only** |
| Long-press | "Hold" — matches the in-app tip's wording |
| Remove | **Remove from match** |
| Fairness impact | **Playing time impact** |

**Still-accurate internal terms** that also appear on screen and are safe to use:
Tinker / Tinkering, LOCKED, Rotate keeper?, ↻ Recalculate rest of match,
Hide score, Review the plan, 📋 Review all plans.

### Product observations (not video problems)

Two things the sweep turned up that are worth a look in the app itself:

1. ~~**The serious fairness warning is effectively dead copy.**~~ **Fixed
   2026-09-11.** `setup-form.js` used to overwrite `#fairness-warning`'s caution
   text with a random quip on first reveal, so a coach sliding into the
   competitive zone saw "Steady on, Pep" and never "Not recommended for
   grassroots — some players will get noticeably less pitch time." Both now
   render: caution in `.fairness-warning-caution`, quip beneath it in
   `.fairness-warning-quip`. The knockout slider ships an empty caution on
   purpose (being competitive for a final is the point of that control), so it
   stays quip-only.
2. **"Rotate keeper?" is the one place "specialist" still shows** ("A specialist
   keeper rotates out so everyone gets equal time"). Harmless, but it's now the
   only surviving trace of the old tier vocabulary in the UI — worth deciding
   whether to keep it or reword to "dedicated keeper" for consistency.
